What still holds from the research

The brain model

Three kinds of memory: an Inbox that fades, the Board as shared working memory, and deep Memory with typed links. People never see tier names; the Digest is the home screen.

Postgres is the truth, md is the view

Pathly's own history showed md files drifting from the database. Items have markdown bodies, frontmatter is the row, and a git export keeps the brain yours.

Links on a leash

Agents propose typed links with a reason and a source. They become real when you keep them or retrieval uses them.

Brain science

ACT-R activation instead of decay, provenance against false memories, a packet of about four chunks, selective sleep, if-then intentions and fan-damped spreading.
